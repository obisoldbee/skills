- **一句话规则**：餐时大量饮水会稀释胃酸 / 胆汁 / 消化酶，降低消化效率，并物理占胃导致吃不下肉；推荐"餐前 2 小时、餐后 2 小时不大量喝水"。OMAD 模式下容易做到，三餐模式较难。
- **证据（一手）**：

  - `[20231117001.md 01:13:15] "they just basically don't drink water for two hours before or after your meal especially if you're doing sort of one meal a day easier to do"`

## 启发式 64：临床视角决策 — "B12 below 500 pmol/L = demyelination + brain atrophy; target above 800-1200"
- **一句话规则**：B12 不足 = 神经脱髓鞘 + 脑萎缩。Chaffee 给出**精确阈值**：(a) **< 500 pmol/L（约 < 650 pg/mL）= demyelination 风险**；(b) Oxford 2008 数据显示此范围人群**脑体积每 5 年缩小 2.5-5.2%**（线性，年化 0.5-1%）；(c) "optimal" 阈值 = 800-1200 pmol/L（1100-1600 pg/mL）；(d) 日本部分研究把"正常"线设在 500+。**Lab reference range 整体偏低**，是 vegetarian / hyper-vegetarian 人群拉低的"假正常"。
- **证据（一手）**：
  - `[20260401001.md 00:00:02] "if your B12 is below 500 picomoles per liter, which is roughly 650 picograms per milliliter in the US, that you can actually get demyelination of your axons"`

  - `[20260401001.md 00:02:39] "80-year-old patient with Alzheimer's, well, with dementia. And then with daily B12 replacement, after 5 months, it looked like a 40-year-old brain"`
  - `[20260518001.md 00:12:13] "they actually show it above 800 or even 800 to 1200 picomoles per liter, which in American terms translates to about 1,100 to 1,600 picograms per milliliter"`

- **vs R1-R3**：R1-R3 只给"测 B12"但无阈值；R4 给出完整阈值表 + 机制（demyelination + brain atrophy）+ 国别差异（Japan vs US）。

## 启发式 65：临床视角决策 — "Change lab reference ranges from population averages to OPTIMAL ranges (not just in-range = fine)"
- **一句话规则**：当前 lab reference range 是"前 N 个 walk-in 病人的 average" — 既然平均人群已 hyper-vegetarian + malnourished + chronic disease，那么 99% 的"normal"实际是 sub-optimal。决策推论：**(a) 单独 B12 正常不代表足够；(b) 同一患者同一指标在不同 lab 可能给出完全相反结论；(c) 应采用 functional medicine 的 optimal range**。
- **证据（一手）**：
