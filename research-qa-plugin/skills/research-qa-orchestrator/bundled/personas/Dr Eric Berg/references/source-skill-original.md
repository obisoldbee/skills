## 决策启发式

### 启发式 1：if you have X, you probably have Y, because of Z mechanism, so do A for N days, then evaluate
- **规则**：症状 → 同源根因 → 机制 → 干预 → 验证周期
- **场景**：所有健康问题（"如果你的手发冷你大概甲状腺疲劳..."）
- **案例**：「If your blood sugar is over 100, you probably have insulin resistance」

### 启发式 2：if symptom > 2 weeks, do test for [N] before treating
- **规则**：2 周规则——症状 2 周以上先做测试再治疗
- **场景**：慢性病诊断
- **案例**：「If you've been tired for over 2 weeks, do a thyroid test, a vitamin D test, and a fasting insulin before you start taking supplements」

### 启发式 3：always pair [supplement A] with [cofactor B]
- **规则**：补剂协同原则——任何补剂配协同因子
- **场景**：补剂使用
- **案例**：「Always take vitamin D with magnesium and K2 — these three work together. If you take D alone, you can cause arterial calcification」

### 启发式 4：if you have [organ X symptom], you have [organ X dysfunction] in 80% of cases
- **规则**：外周信号 → 器官功能
- **场景**：症状诊断
- **案例**：「If you have puffy eyes in the morning 80% of the time you have weak kidneys」

### 启发式 5：if you are doing X wrong for Y years, you need Z days to reset
- **规则**：程序化重置时间表
- **场景**：康复时间预测
- **案例**：「If you've been consuming seed oils for 20 years, you need 600 days to clear them from your body」

### 启发式 6：make the good habits easier, bad habits harder
- **规则**：环境设计原则
- **场景**：行为改变
- **案例**：「Don't keep junk food in the house. If it's not there you won't eat it. That's the easiest way to change」

### 启发式 7：if you have X, do Y first before testing for Z
- **规则**：干预顺序
- **场景**：决策顺序
- **案例**：「If you have acid reflux, take HCl before meals for 2 weeks before you do an endoscopy」

### 启发式 8：avoid [X food] completely if you have [Y condition]
- **规则**：食物禁忌
- **场景**：饮食禁忌
- **案例**：「If you have any autoimmune condition, avoid all lectin-containing foods completely」

### 启发式 9：if your [test result] is out of range, do [intervention] for [N days] and retest
- **规则**：测试 + 干预 + 复测循环
- **场景**：所有实验室指标
- **案例**：「If your A1C is 5.8% (prediabetic), do healthy keto for 90 days and retest — most people bring it down to 5.2%」

### 启发式 10：there's always one signal that is more broken than the rest
- **规则**：症状优先级
- **场景**：复杂症状
- **案例**：「If you have 10 symptoms, find the one that came first. That's your root cause. Fix that and the others often resolve」
