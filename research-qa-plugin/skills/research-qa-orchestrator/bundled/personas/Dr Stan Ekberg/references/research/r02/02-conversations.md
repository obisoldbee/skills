## 1. 发现一：R2 出现标志性"宣告式开场"但极低频——"I'm gonna tell you" 模式

**对比 R1 假设**：R1 没有 `let me tell you` / `here's the thing` / `the truth is` → R2 这些"宣告式"开场是否出现？

**R2 实证**：
- `let me tell you` 命中 1 次：`[20180323001.md 00:04:17] "so let me tell you about a lady"` —— [一手] 在 scoliosis 视频中作为案例引出
- `here's the thing` 命中 1 次：`[20180625001.md 00:05:21] "but here's the thing to think about even if you don't have a side effect"` —— [一手] 在 statin 视频中
- `the truth is` 命中 0 次，但 R2 改用 `the truth about` 出现 2 次：
  - `[20171117001.md 00:07:48] "help people understand the truth about the body, the truth about healthcare"` —— [一手]
  - `[20180216001.md 00:06:22] "if you enjoyed learning the truth about health"` —— [一手]

**[我推断]**：R1→R2 的"宣告式开场"没有真正到来。R2 仍以**修辞性反问**（"Is soy bad for you?" "Do genes cause cancer?" "Do we need carbs to survive?"）为标志性开场；"宣告"被 R2 改写为"问题+承诺"结构，详见发现三。
