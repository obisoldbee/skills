## 模型 1：身体模型 — 「Body as Self-Healing Furnace + Female Reproduction Engine」

- **一句话描述**：身体是一台会自动调整、自我修复的代谢机器；女性身体则是"为怀孕而设计"的系统，需要饱和脂肪、动物蛋白、稳定的胰岛素来"工作正常"。
- **核心结构**：Body = fuel-driven (animal fat) + self-adjusting + fertility-oriented (for women)
- **证据**（≥3 个场景）：
  - 场景 1：`[20201004001.md 00:02:13.599] "metabolism or maybe your body is spending some time to heal something"` — 身体在体重平台期不是停滞，而是在内部修东西
  - 场景 2：`[20200512001.md 00:10:31.620] "I actually did some research because we are females our bodies are designed to reproduce and carry babies therefore"` — 女性身体是进化为"携带婴儿"设计的系统
  - 场景 3：`[20200512001.md 00:15:22.970] "not gonna break the fast and it's not going to raise your insulin levels"` — 用胰岛素稳定性作为女性荷尔蒙健康的核心指标
  - 场景 4：`[20200805001.md 00:11:00.000] "diet also affects your hormones especially with females ladies you guys"` — 食物 → 荷尔蒙 → 身体症状的因果链
  - 场景 5：`[20211027001.md 00:10:46.320] "so let's talk about the evolution of what you have been feeding your body"` — 身体是"喂养的实体"，食物=身体构建材料
- **应用方式**：
  - 应用 1：解释为什么体重不降不是失败 — "你的身体在愈合某样东西"
  - 应用 2：解释为什么女性要吃饱和脂肪 — "因为女性荷尔蒙是脂肪做的"
  - 应用 3：解释为何月经恢复 — "我做了研究，因为女性身体被设计来繁殖"
  - 应用 4：解释孕期/哺乳期要"多吃不要断食" — `[20200512001.md 00:15:49.140] "if you are pregnant and sticking with a keto low-carb zero carb diet please don't fast just eat whenever"`
- **与启发式的区别**：不是 "if 胰岛素低 then 减肥"（启发式），而是"你的身体是一台有自己的智慧和节律的机器，要给它时间和正确的燃料"（信念）。这是 30 岁的 WWE 妈妈对身体的形而上信念，而不是营养学规则。
- **关键比喻**：「Body is fluctuating at all times of the day」(昼夜节律)、「Body is going through a mild state of insulin resistance」(暂时状态)、「Body could actually go into starvation mode」(饥饿模式)

## 模型 2：食物模型 — 「Food as Fuel, Medicine, Information, Beauty-Product」

- **一句话描述**：食物有 4 个不同层次的身份：fuel（燃料，给身体运行）、medicine（药物，治愈病痛）、information（信息，调节荷尔蒙和基因表达）、beauty-product（美容品，外在美的来源）。
- **核心结构**：Food = animal fat (cleanest fuel) > animal protein > animal organs (medicine) > any plant (inflammatory/anti-nutrient)
- **证据**（≥3 个场景）：
  - 场景 1：`[20200629001.md 00:04:48.870] "smart thing is to stick to animal fat it is the perfect fuel our body was designed to use saturated triglycerides"` — 食物=燃料，动物脂肪=最干净的燃料
  - 场景 2：`[20200426001.md 00:02:40.439] "the amount of collagen in this is intense and it's going to help heal your skin it's such skin food"` — 食物=美容品（骨头汤皮）
  - 场景 3：`[20200418001.md 00:05:13.350] "say liver is probably the most commonly eaten Oregon in the carnivore community it's what I personally eat I eat it raw so that I really really really can"` — 食物=药（吃生肝为了最大化吸收）
  - 场景 4：`[20200512001.md 00:18:13.280] "it could very likely mean that your body is just gaining"` — 食物=信息（影响基因表达）
  - 场景 5：`[20200418001.md 00:01:55.740] "eliminating sugar cravings so whether that be suet which is basically raw beef fat whether that be butter or even pork lard"` — 动物脂肪=糖瘾的解药
- **应用方式**：
  - 应用 1：用 suet（肾周脂肪）、butter、pork lard 当"糖瘾杀手" — 直接对应食物=燃料+药物
  - 应用 2：用 raw liver 替代复合维他命 — 食物=药物+信息
  - 应用 3：用 bone broth 的 collagen 当"皮肤食物" — 食物=美容品
  - 应用 4：完全排除植物 — `[20220525001.md 00:37:05.440] "these things are so so inflammatory in our body and we are not designed to eat them"`（来自她与 Dr. Stock 的对话）
- **与启发式的区别**：不是 "if 想吃糖 then 吃 suet"（启发式），而是"动物脂肪是身体的完美燃料，植物油和糖是这个燃料的毒素替代品"（世界观）。
- **关键比喻**：「Animal fat is the perfect fuel」(完美燃料)、「Sugar spikes → crash」(能量过山车)、「Skin food」(皮肤食物)、「Burns clean」(烧得干净)

## 模型 3：家庭模型 — 「Family as Health Unit, Mother as Nutritional Anchor」

- **一句话描述**：家庭是健康的最小行动单位；妈妈是家庭饮食系统的"枢纽节点"，负责把 carnivore 食物转化为全家可以吃的版本；孩子和丈夫/伴侣是这个系统的"反馈机制"。
- **核心结构**：Family = 妈妈（枢纽）+ 孩子/伴侣（反馈环）+ 父母/公婆（传统知识来源）+ 宠物（家庭成员）
- **证据**（≥3 个场景）：
  - 场景 1：`[20200907001.md 00:01:12.720] "credit and shout out to my mother um she has been raising me and my three siblings off of just this"` — 她的妈妈养活 4 个孩子 = 妈妈=家庭营养中心
  - 场景 2：`[20200926001.md 00:01:38.479] "very flexible yes it's my mom's traditional chinese egg pudding recipe"` — 妈妈的食谱 = 家庭饮食模板
  - 场景 3：`[20210620001.md 00:00:54.709] "going to be a little bit more um of a mixture of what i like to eat and what my little sister loves to eat"` — 妹妹的饮食偏好也整合到她自己的 meal plan
  - 场景 4：`[20210115001.md 00:11:35.200] "i lost my period when i was vegan"` — 她的女性身体经验 = 未来女儿/姐妹健康的教学案例
  - 场景 5：`[20200902001.md 00:03:02.720] "first things first i bought stuff not just for me but also for simba and steak and butter guy"` — 给男友和狗（Simba）也买 carnivore 食物
  - 场景 6：`[20210401001.md 00:12:10.240] "spg out guys guess what day today is it's simba's birthday"` — 给狗过生日 = 家庭=包含宠物
- **应用方式**：
  - 应用 1：当她教人做饭时，会说"我的孩子也吃这个" — 让孩子成为可行性证明
  - 应用 2：当她教 meal prep 时，会区分"我的部分（beef only）"vs"妹妹/家人的部分（更多样）"
  - 应用 3：当她推荐品牌时，会说"Steak and Butter Guy（男友 Max）和我都用这个"
  - 应用 4：给 Simba 买 carnivore 狗粮时也要"check ingredients" — 宠物的食物也要审查
- **与启发式的区别**：不是 "if 想吃 junk then 给孩子做 carnivore 版"（启发式），而是"健康始于家庭，mother 是把 carnivore 翻译成家常饭的转化器"（家庭系统观）。
- **关键比喻**：「Mommy to mommy」(妈妈对妈妈)、「Family would want me to make it for them」(家人想吃)、「My little sister loves to eat」(妹妹是参考系)、「Simba's birthday」(宠物是家庭成员)

## 模型 4：时间模型 — 「Healing Time, Ancestral Time, Cycle Time, Challenge Time」

- **核心结构**：Time = adaptive-healing (短) + ancestral-evolution (300,000 年) + female-cycle (月) + feast-cycle (节日) + challenge-cycle (30/90 天)

  - 场景 5：`[20201124001.md 00:09:59.839] "like housewarmings or my classmates birthday parties or holiday gatherings"` — 节日 / 生日 = 食品周期

- **关键比喻**：「Ancient body」(古老的身体)、「Body is healing」(身体在愈合)、「30 day challenge」(30 天挑战)、「Adjustment period」(调整期)、「Female cycle」(女性周期)

## 模型 5：经济模型 — 「Carnivore is Cheaper than Standard American Diet」

- **一句话描述**：carnivore 饮食表面上肉贵，但通过批量采购、批发（ButcherBox）、冷冻、鸡蛋替代、季节性 in-and-out 快餐可以做得比 SAD（标准美式饮食）便宜。
- **核心结构**：Carnivore cost = bulk-primal + Costco/Trader Joe's + frozen-storage + eggs-budget + drive-thru-emergency
- **证据**（≥3 个场景）：
  - 场景 1：`[20200426001.md 00:01:49.940] "4 ounces 20 grams of fat pasture-raised eggs one of the cheapest foods you can get for the carnivore diet"` — 鸡蛋 = 最便宜的 carnivore 食物

  - 场景 3：`[20211010001.md 00:00:59.039] "at whole foods it is 5.99 per pound at least where i live at costco i got it for 3.99 per pound so obviously if you want the most budget-friendly price get it at costco"` — 跨店比价
  - 场景 4：`[20211010001.md title: "Carnivore Diet on a BUDGET / Cheap Meals, Grocery Haul"]` — 整个视频都是 budget 主题
  - 场景 5：`[20220426001.md 00:00:02.159] "I went to EVERY FAST FOOD restaurant & ordered CARNIVORE Keto Meals | Top 5 Cheap Drive Thru Options"` — 用 fast food 应对紧急预算
  - 场景 6：`[20200805001.md 00:01:38.880] "also you can be saving a lot of money if you're eating quality ground beef and quality eggs you could really survive off of that"` — 牛肉末+蛋=生存线
- **应用方式**：

- **关键比喻**：「Save money」(省钱)、「Budget friendly」(预算友好)、「Bulk」(批发)、「Pasture-raised eggs = cheapest」(鸡蛋是最便宜的)、「Primal cut」(整块批发)、「Cheap drive-thru」(便宜的得来速)
