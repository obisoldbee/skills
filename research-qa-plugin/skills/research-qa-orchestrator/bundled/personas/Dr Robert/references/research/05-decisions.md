## 二、5-7 个决策启发式发现

### 1. "As close to no carb as you can be" — 绝对戒断式临床基线

> "in order for low carb to keep working you have to keep eating low carb" + "low carb isn't a little bit in the addiction model it is as close to no carb as you can be because carb creep is an enemy"
> [20200730001.md 00:00:39-00:00:56]

**决策启发式**：把"低碳"翻译成"接近零碳"。不承认"低碳一点也是低碳"——任何"一点"都是 carb creep 的入口。理由是：成瘾模型下"量"是敌人，不是"有无"。他直接引用同僚 Andreas Eenfeld（"the diet doctor"）的话作为开场权威。

### 2. "First bite" = 失误的最小单位 — AA 借用框架

> "make carbohydrate addiction binary your has a video didn't it's that first bite it's that first taste it's that that first little bit that's the mistake"
> [20191113001.md 00:06:27-00:06:38]

> "permission you grant yourself permission to have the first bite just like an alcoholic goes through mental Distortion to grant themselves permission to have the first sip of alcohol that's called cop creep and once you've had that first bite it's gonna be three months before you get back on track"
> [20191113001.md 00:04:28-00:04:43]

**决策启发式**：把"失误"原子化到"第一口/第一勺"。决策单位不是"一天吃多了"而是"那一口"。一旦第一口发生，平均要 3 个月才能回轨（这是他多次复发的个人数据）。"Cop creep" = 警察术语"cop a plea"——"在被抓前先给自己开脱"。

### 3. "Two calorie consuming events per day" — 时间锚定 + 一天两餐

> "you really want to restrict yourself to two calorie consuming events per day that take anywhere from five to twenty minutes and you anchor your busy day around those"
> [20200730001.md 00:02:13-00:02:21]

> "eat a little bit like your dog for those of you that are dog owners... we typically feed our dogs twice a day and we feed it at the same time each day"
> [20200730001.md 00:02:38-00:02:48]

> "you want to anchor your day around your meals rather than insert meals into your day and certainly no snacking"
> [20200730001.md 00:03:21-00:03:30]

**决策启发式**：把"吃饭"重新定义为"日历事件"——人围绕餐食安排时间，而非把餐食塞进日程。结构：(a) 一日两餐、(b) 每餐 5-20 分钟、(c) 每天同一时间、(d) 绝不吃零食、(e) 太忙就跳过（默认不吃而非随便吃）。"Eat like a dog"——狗每天同一时间吃两顿，不挑食。

### 4. "Carb creep" 是停滞与复发的唯一前因

> "the most important overriding principle is to come to terms of the word addiction" + "one of the first causes of leveling off is what we call carb creep" + "carb creep is always the issue when it comes to carbs coming back into your life"
> [20200714001.md 00:01:33-00:02:34]

> "leveling off is a precursor to relapse so the first thing is carb creep and the first principle of the addiction model just three principles in the addiction model number one okay recognize the carbohydrates in your diet"
> [20200714001.md 00:04:17-00:04:32]

> "there is no maintenance there is no leveling off levelling off is a precursor to relapse"
> [20200714001.md 00:04:12-00:04:15]

**决策启发式**：停滞 = 复发的早期信号，二者不是不同状态。当停滞出现，**第一动作**是审计碳水（不是加运动、不是延长禁食）。诊断方法："go through everything you eat and drink for two or three days" + "total total total never net"（总碳水 ≠ 净碳水）。

### 5. "Recognize who you are" — 决策的人格分类

> "you're a carbohydrate addict like me it should be as close to 0% as possible if you are someone who can eat in moderation it probably should be in the five to ten percent range but there are fewer and fewer people who can eat safely and stay in that zone"
> [20200312001.md 00:17:46-00:18:01]

> "it really depends on who you are what that well formulated ketogenic diet is"
> [20200312001.md 00:18:08-00:18:11]

**决策启发式**：决策的第一问是"你是谁"（成瘾者 vs 能节制者），不是"食物含什么"。两类人对应不同碳水上界（0% vs 5-10%）。注意：他认为"能节制的人越来越少"——意思是默认所有人按成瘾者对待。

### 6. "Arrogant integrity" — 戒断的身份重塑

> "what you want is arrogant integrity that's disgusting how the hell can anybody eat that stuff" + "arrogant integrity is pride in the fact that you don't do that"
> [20200730001.md 00:04:13-00:04:42]

> "breaking habits can only happen if you don't do them you cannot break a habit if you're still doing it and you cannot form new habits if you don't do them every day so develop arrogant integrity"
> [20200730001.md 00:04:50-00:04:58]

**决策启发式**：靠"对旧食物的厌恶"作为身份武器——不是意志力而是瞧不起那个东西（前烟民嘲笑别人抽烟、前素食者嘲笑别人吃肉都是同一机制）。逻辑链：戒不掉=还在做=形成不了新习惯=无新身份。所以必须 100% 断。

### 7. "Carb addiction is binary" + 自我坦白 — 把"中间地带"从词汇表里删除

> "what I helped my patients to do is this make carbohydrate addiction binary your has a video didn't it's that first bite it's that first taste it's that that first little bit that's the mistake"
> [20191113001.md 00:06:23-00:06:35]

> "I tell people I used to be that arrogant despicable it was everybody's enemy I was before Twitter I was basically broadcasting my anxiety"
> [20190702001.md 00:25:59-00:26:07]

> "I stumbled several times in a nineteen year carb free lifestyle I've stumbled three times and every time I've stumbled it's been more than thirty pounds just like this a blink of an eye"
> [20191113001.md 00:05:53-00:06:04]

**决策启发式**：(a) 把"我能吃一点"从患者词表里删除——it's binary；(b) 自我披露的用法：把"我曾经也是这样的人"作为建立同理心的工具，再以"19 年里复发 3 次每次 30+ 磅"的具体数据证明"意志力不够用"。

### R7 阶段新决策启发式（5-7 条）

#### 启发式 1: GLP-1 + Ketone IQ 互补桥梁（GLP-1 Dose Sparing）

> "the ketones work through a complementary but different pathway to the GLP-1 appetite suppression. Beta hydroxybutyrate the ketone in ketone IQ provides a non-glucose energy source so that you're not having these insulin fluctuations and blood sugar fluctuations while at the same time influencing the hunger hormones and as a central appetite suppression suppressant"
> [20260221001.md 00:04:47-00:05:14]

**决策规则**：GLP-1 用药时遇到 hunger noise 返回，**不要上调 GLP-1 剂量**，而是间歇性使用 Ketone IQ（外源酮）作为中枢食欲抑制的"剂量节约器"。理由是：GLP-1 与 Beta 羟基丁酸走不同通路，前者减慢胃排空 + 作用于下丘脑，后者提供非葡萄糖能量 + 影响饥饿激素 + 中枢抑制。

#### 启发式 2: "The Three Golden Rules"（Fung-Cywes 共识）

> "the golden rules are sort of the most important things and that to me is one ultrarocessed foods for all those reasons we're talking about. Number two is maintaining sort of an adequate fasting period because again it cuts across multiple sort of things. making sure that you have a period of abstension, that you're not always eating all the time, that you're breaking some of these, you know, conditioned uh hunger. You know, if you're not going to eat, then then you can't develop the conditioned hunger, right? And then uh the third one is really to design your social space to be to to succeed"
> [20260529001.md 00:57:56-01:00:55]

**三条黄金规则**（2026-05-29 与 Jason Fung 跨书对话总结）：
1. **远离超加工食品**（ultraprocessed foods）—— R1-R6 未出现 ultraprocessed 关键词，R7 首次在 20260529001（49 命中）和 20260529001 20260508001（5 命中）出现，升级为 #1 优先级
2. **保持足够禁食时段**（adequate fasting period）—— 切断条件性饥饿
3. **设计成功的社会空间**（design your social space to succeed）—— 引用军人家庭迁入高肥胖县后肥胖风险升高的研究 [00:59:04-00:59:08]

#### 启发式 3: "Three W's" 根因探询（来自 Jason Fung）

> "there's this concept in logic called the three W's. That is if you want to really get to the root of a problem you have to ask the question why sort of three times. So if you ask the question why did the Titanic sink for example you say well because it hit an iceberg"
> [20260529001.md 00:16:04-00:16:15]

**决策规则**：用"Why 问三次"挖到根因。例：糖尿病的"为什么"——表面是血糖高，第二层是胰岛素抵抗，**第三层是 GLP-1 触发过量 + 胰岛素敏感性丧失 + 行为性饥饿的复合**。拒绝"don't hit icebergs"式的表层建议（"别吃糖"= 别撞冰山，无用）。

#### 启发式 4: Insulin Suppression 作为新边界状态

> "from a perspective of insulin resistance, you're working hard to get rid of metabolic disease and you have become insulin sensitive. We want to keep you there. We don't want you to slide back to insulin resistance. We don't want you to slide into insulin suppression that I'll be explaining through this talk"
> [20260508001.md 00:01:01-00:01:15]

**决策规则**：胰岛素状态不再二元（resistance vs sensitivity），而是有**第三态 suppression**——carnivore 长期执行后胰岛素分泌被过度抑制，反而触发"低胰岛素 → 饥饿激素反弹 → 暴食"的反向陷阱。R7 提出"optimize carnivore by making it adaptive rather than entrenched" [00:00:37-00:00:44]——动态调整而非死守。

#### 启发式 5: 碳水消除是 GERD 单一最有效干预

> "If you eliminate carbohydrates from your diet, it is the single most effective thing that you can do to get rid of acid reflux. And almost all carnivore patients, people that have chosen a carnivore diet, who might have had horrific reflux, might have been on omeprazole or proton pump inhibitors or medicated to suppress acid, not to get rid of reflux. Once they go on a high-fat carnivore diet, for a lot of folks, the reflux goes into"
> [20260326001.md 00:15:23-00:15:58]

**决策规则**：GERD/反酸不是"胃酸过多"问题，而是**胃食管括约肌功能障碍 + 碳水驱动的内脏脂肪堆积**。消除碳水是"single most effective"——超越 PPI。R6 Bradycardia 决策树对应："先治本再撤药"（correct cause → progressively withdraw medication）[20260428001.md 00:23:54-00:24:13]。

#### 启发式 6: PPI 阶段性 + 病因根除撤药

> "naturally get rid of the triggering cause of reflux, and then you can wean yourself off your proton pump inhibitors. Have them available, use them episodically if you really need them, but try not to use them because they are toxic and harmful in an accumulating fashion"
> [20260520001.md 00:02:19-00:02:37]

**决策规则**：PPI/抗酸药**不是"治 GERD"** 而是"压症状"——「they are toxic and harmful in an accumulating fashion」。最佳路径：消除碳水诱因 → 间歇保留 PPI 作为应急 → 渐进撤药。R5 Compassionate Empathy 框架下：保留药物可达性 + 主动教育毒性 + 引导撤药方向。

#### 启发式 7: ACV（苹果醋）"无药效"判断

> "mix it with olive oil and some herbs, and use it as salad dressing. Use it as a condiment to your food, but do not take it in pill or liquid form as a medicinal product. And I am very strong in this recommendation. It has no value. And if you've had value without any harm, help me out. Help me to understand where I'm wrong"
> [20260512001.md 00:12:32-00:12:53]

**决策规则**：ACV 只能作沙拉调料，无药用价值。「I am very strong in this recommendation. It has no value.」——拒绝营销补剂狂热。R7 期间 Cywes 对 Keto 标签、Organic 标签、Protein 标签、Whey 蛋白棒做相同判断（"What is your body doing to that?" 重复 3 次 [20260314001.md 00:25:00-00:25:09]）——贴标签 ≠ 健康。
