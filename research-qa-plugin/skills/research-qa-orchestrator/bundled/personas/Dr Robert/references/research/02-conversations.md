## 二、对话维度的关键发现

### 发现 1 — 顿悟链 `I realized` 反复出现，且结构高度一致

Cywes 不是单次顿悟，而是把同一段"我曾经 300 磅 → 试遍所有 diet → 钱包瘦了 → 然后我意识到…" 在不同访谈里反复讲。

- "what I realized I tried every strategy out there I tried a bunch of diets I spent too bunch of money on what I call wallet biopsies" [20190702001.md 00:02:12]
- "what I realized is that instead of addressing my weight I needed to focus on why I gained it in the first place" [20190702001.md 00:02:46]
- "I realized that I'd really been raised and it's nobody's fault... a comprehensive deficit of effective emotion management strategies" [20190702001.md 00:03:52]
- "I realized especially when I kind of quit playing team sport" [20190702001.md 00:03:48]
- "I realized that I started eating sugar and starch forget about the rest of my diet" [20190702001.md 00:05:41]
- "I realized then that we got it completely wrong" [20190702001.md 00:07:59]
- "I realized it doesn't matter if it's stone cut oatmeal it doesn't matter if it's ice cream sugar is sugar by the time it enters your bloodstream" [20190702001.md 00:13:28]
- "in the 1990s I realized in the work I was doing in Toronto with my..." [20190710001.md 00:11:59]

模式：每次"realized"都对应一个因果链（行为 → 失败 → 情绪根源）。不是单一时刻的顿悟，而是同一段"我曾"的递归。

### 发现 2 — Wounded Healer 框架：体重 + 性格 + 治疗师三联自陈

Cywes 的"wounded healer"不在字面（grep 0 命中），但三件套在 20190702001.md 中全部出现，且做了一次"对自己今晚"的元评论：

- 体重："I topped out at around 300 pounds" [20190702001.md 00:01:46]
- 行为："I was basically broadcasting my anxiety and everybody... that's why it used to be" [20190702001.md 00:26:07]
- 性格："I tell people I used to be that arrogant despicable it was everybody's enemy I was before Twitter" [20190702001.md 00:26:01]
- 元评论："I've done something tonight that I just cannot believe that I either and I did good affair I feel comfortably now but I cannot believe that I did" [20190702001.md 00:25:53]

→ "今晚"他在录制时主动讲了自己曾是"广播焦虑的混蛋"。这种"治疗师在镜头前脱壳"是高密度 wounded healer 信号。

- "that's called cop creep and once you've had that first bite it's gonna be three months before you get back on track" [20191113001.md 00:04:43]
- "make carbohydrate addiction binary... it's that first bite it's that first taste it's that first little bit that's the mistake" [20191113001.md 00:06:30]
