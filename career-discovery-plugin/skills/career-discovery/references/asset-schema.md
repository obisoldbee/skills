## Role record

Store role dates, title, organization (or anonymized label), industry, team context, formal responsibilities, actual recurring work, decision boundaries, projects, transitions, and source notes.

## Capability record

For each capability:
- name and plain-language definition;
- supporting projects or situations;
- observed behavior;
- evidence status;
- confidence;
- counterexamples or limits;
- user confirmation.

## Claim ledger

Each material claim stores exact wording, status, source, context, ownership, caveat, and approval state. Never upgrade an inferred claim without confirmation.
