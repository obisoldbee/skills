# Job Description Mapping

## Workflow

1. Parse the JD into responsibilities, required capabilities, preferred capabilities, seniority signals, and domain context.
2. Separate explicit requirements from inferred expectations.
3. Map each requirement to confirmed career assets.
4. Classify evidence:
   - `strong`: direct and substantial evidence;
   - `partial`: relevant but incomplete evidence;
   - `adjacent`: transferable evidence from a different context;
   - `absent`: no supporting evidence found;
   - `unclear`: more interview work needed.
5. Select the strongest matching stories.
6. Identify real gaps and decide whether further interview questions could resolve them.
7. Tailor the resume by changing emphasis and ordering, not by changing facts.

## Output

| JD requirement | Evidence asset | Match | Gap / follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|

Do not claim a skill solely because the JD contains the keyword. If the user has adjacent experience, describe the transfer honestly.
